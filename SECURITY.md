# Security policy

OpenPak runs accounts, console links and game sessions for real people, so security reports
come first.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Report it privately. Do not open a public issue, pull request or discussion.

- Preferred: GitHub's **Report a vulnerability** button on the affected repository's Security tab
  (private vulnerability reporting).
- Or email [contact@openpak.org](mailto:contact@openpak.org) with "Security" in the subject.

Please include the affected service or repository, what an attacker can do, and the steps to
reproduce. A proof of concept against your own account is fine. Do not access other people's
accounts or data, do not degrade the service for others, and do not keep data you come across.

We will acknowledge a report within a week. We'll keep you informed while we fix it and credit
you when the fix ships, unless you would rather stay anonymous.

## Scope

In scope: every repository in this organisation and the services at `openpak.org` and its
subdomains.

Out of scope: the consoles' own firmware and the games themselves, upstream projects we fork
(report to them; tell us too if our fork is affected), denial-of-service tests and social
engineering.

## Supported versions

Only the latest release of each service, and what currently runs on openpak.org, are supported.
OpenPak is in alpha; there are no long-term branches.
