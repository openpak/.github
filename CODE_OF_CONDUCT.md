# Code of Conduct

OpenPak is for anyone who wants their consoles online again. Be someone people are glad to
play with.

## Expected

- Be respectful and patient, especially with newcomers and people reporting problems.
- Disagree about ideas, not people.
- Assume good faith and help each other get to the answer.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, threats, slurs, or demeaning comments about anyone's identity or background.
- Sexual content or advances, or doxxing (sharing someone's private information).
- Cheating tools, account theft, or attacks against other players or the network.
- Sharing or asking for pirated games, firmware, keys or console dumps.
- Spam, or repeatedly derailing a conversation after being asked to stop.

This applies in every OpenPak space: repositories, issues, pull requests, the website, in-game
interactions on the network, and any community channels.

## Enforcement

Report problems to [contact@openpak.org](mailto:contact@openpak.org). Reports are handled
privately. Maintainers may remove content, lock threads, or ban people from the repositories
and from the network, temporarily or permanently, depending on what happened.
